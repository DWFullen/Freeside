## Decision

No adult titles are listed at launch. Adult-classified submissions are held, not rejected. Listing opens when the `project.md` §0.4 exit criteria are met, and does not wait for Strike or the ACH provider to accept adult content. Review no later than 6 months after launch; missing the review date requires a public notice.
